About Us

Welcome to GM Service Catering (GM Service Caterers), Indore's premier pure vegetarian catering and royal crockery service located Near Nariman City, Chhota Bangarda, Indore. Driven by an authentic passion for rich taste, culinary tradition, and royal hospitality, we curate unforgettable dining experiences for every event.

Whether you are hosting an intimate gathering of 25 to 50 persons or organizing a grand celebration for 10,000+ guests, our professional team delivers exquisite presentations, bespoke menus, royal crockery, and hygienic service crafted to perfection.

Our Specialized Catering & Tableware Solutions:
• Events & Functions: Caterers for Weddings, Pre-Wedding Sangeet & Mehendi, Birthday Parties, Kitty Parties, Family Celebrations, and Corporate Events.
• Royal Crockery & Designer Tableware: Exclusive 24K gold and silver-plated thalis, designer bone-china, copper & brass chafing setups, and luxury glassware for events of all scales starting from ₹10,000.
• 8 Authentic Regional Cuisines: Rajasthani, Marwari, Punjabi, North Indian, Gujarati, Sindhi, Maharashtrian, and Chinese prepared with genuine regional spices.
• 100% Pure Vegetarian & Dedicated Jain Cookware: Prepared with pure cow desi ghee, dedicated separate utensils for Jain catering, zero root vegetables, and strict onion-garlic-free choices upon request.
• Theatrical Live Culinary Counters: World-famous Indori Sarafa live chaat stations, sizzling live tawa specialties, and artisanal live pasta woks.
• Royal Buffet Setups: Opulent hammered brass and copper chafing presentations, illuminated buffet counters, and courteous stewards for seamless hospitality.
